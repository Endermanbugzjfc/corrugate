# Corrugate

Corrugate associates saved visuals with explanations in a Codex conversation.

## Language

**Whiteboard**:
The collection of visual items belonging to one conversation.
_Avoid_: Canvas, transcript

**Item**:
A saved visual and its description, linked to an explanation in the conversation.
_Avoid_: Attachment, message

**Card**:
The visible presentation of an item in the whiteboard.
_Avoid_: Item (when referring specifically to its on-screen presentation)

**Conversation anchor**:
The identifiable point in a conversation to which an item refers.
_Avoid_: Line number

**Attachment**:
The association between a whiteboard and the terminal pane showing its conversation.
_Avoid_: Session (when referring to a temporary terminal association)
